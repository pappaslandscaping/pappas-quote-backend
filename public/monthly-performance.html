Customer pricing targets

Set the minimum pre-tax visit value you want for each budgeted visit hour for each customer. This is the planned time for the whole crew on site, not one hour per worker. For example, a $150 target means a half-hour visit needs a $75 pre-tax price. Targets are saved in YardDesk and never change customer prices.

This is a planning goal, not profit. Materials, travel, equipment, overhead, and actual labor cost are not included.
